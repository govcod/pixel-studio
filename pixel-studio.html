ABOUT THE STUDIO

Founded on 5 May 2026, PIXEL STUDIO was born from a singular belief — that great hospitality brands deserve world-class visual identities. We are not a generic agency.

We are a design-first creative studio obsessed with the intersection of branding, storytelling, and visual experience. Every café, restaurant, and bar we work with gets a custom visual system built to be remembered.

From the first sip to the last bite — we shape how hospitality brands are seen, felt, and remembered.
